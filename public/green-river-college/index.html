Privacy & limitations

What happens to your résumé

  • Your résumé is used only to generate the report you request.
  • Your résumé may be processed through xAI's Grok API as part of report generation.
  • FixLine does not sell your résumé information or use it for advertising.
  • Uploaded résumés are deleted within 24 hours after processing.
  • FixLine keeps only minimal, non-identifying usage information needed to evaluate the pilot, such as whether a report was completed and whether it was generated self-serve or with an advisor.
  • You may request deletion of information FixLine has retained about you.

What FixLine does not promise

FixLine does not guarantee employment, income, admission, funding, or business success. Recommendations are based on the information you provide and current external research. Treat them as a starting point for your own research, conversations, and judgment — not a promise of outcome.

FixLine is not a job board, admissions service, financial adviser, legal adviser, or substitute for a college counselor. Opportunities, programs, platform rules and eligibility requirements can change, so verify important details directly before relying on them.
